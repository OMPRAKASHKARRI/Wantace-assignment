## Evidence and integrity requirements

### Part A — real-business audit

Part A requires a genuine interview of at least 20 minutes with the owner or manager of a business that makes, processes, or repairs goods. It also requires a process map, two direct quotations in the speaker's original language, photographs of two current tracking methods, and permission to share the business name, area, and owner's phone number with Wantace.

**Do not present AI-generated images, a simulated interview, or the fictional Vardhan Steelware case as real field evidence.** Include only actual photographs, quotations, workflow details, and consent obtained from the business. Blur private customer information and ask permission before photographing or sharing material.

### Parts B–D

Distinguish direct evidence from assumptions or items requiring confirmation. Repeated-looking order rows should be treated as candidates for human review, not confirmed duplicates. Keep kilograms, pieces, dozens, and sets distinct unless a valid conversion is confirmed.

Part D's LinkedIn post must be based on an insight from the candidate's own Part A audit, anonymised. If the real audit has not been completed, revise the post after obtaining a verified observation.

### Part E

The candidate-recorded video should keep the camera on and cover:
1. What surprised the candidate in the Part A visit.
2. The most important thing in the Vardhan pack that the client did not say directly.
3. One decision in the requirements document the candidate would change with another day.

The AI note should identify the tools used, explain their purpose, and include one genuine example of AI giving something wrong or generic and how it was checked or corrected. Remove placeholders before submission.
